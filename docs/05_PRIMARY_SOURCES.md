Live checked 2026-10-04. These five sources support the engineering plan in 02_J_LINK_RESEARCH; published results are not measurements on this host.

| ID | Exact primary source | Verified scope and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| S41 | [Helix: Distributed Serving of Large Language Models via Max-Flow on Heterogeneous GPUs, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.01566v1) | Sections 4-5 describe per-request pipelines and layer placement; real prototype is A100/L4/T4 NVIDIA heterogeneity. Distributed/high-heterogeneity evaluations include simulations. Inference, distributed nodes, not cross-vendor OpenCL or training. |

| S43 | [FlexGen, arXiv:2303.06865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06865) | Single-GPU inference with GPU/CPU/disk tensor placement and batching. Evidence for offload/transfer tradeoffs, not cross-vendor multi-GPU resident pooling. |

Use completed host readback plus destination upload between separate platforms. Advertised SVM is not arbitrary cross-vendor dedicated VRAM. |
